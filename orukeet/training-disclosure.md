# Orukeet R15-0100: checkpoint and exposure disclosure

R15-0100 is the archived ancestor immediately before FT-4035. Its NeMo SHA-256 is `4295a6d820a40b99786331d1c7a6b6c328916c8329b23d39415b0649a5d42811`. It is one standalone FastConformer/TDT checkpoint with the unchanged Parakeet v3 tokenizer and architecture. The recorded dense parameter count is 627,008,134, including 110,592 fixed Gabor coefficients.

This checkpoint predates both the FT-4035 continuation whose training-partition selection used Monsoon English results and the later r3 continuation trained directly on all 2,939 LibriSpeech test-other recordings. Those later weight updates are absent from R15-0100.

**Earlier benchmark exposure remains.** The initial adaptation and cooldown recipes included LibriSpeech test-other among their validation datasets, and saved evaluations cover all 2,939 recordings. Their launch configuration monitors `val_wer` and retains checkpoints. The initial validation logs show that monitored value matching FLEURS validation, with test-other reported separately; this does not establish that test-other controlled automatic checkpoint selection. Whether its observed results influenced selection of the released fixed-step EMA exports has not been established. We do not describe those early checkpoints as unexposed to test-other.

R15's subsequent Gabor-recovery selection used an existing regression suite containing all 2,620 LibriSpeech test-clean recordings, plus VoxPopuli and other evaluation subsets. The exact overlap between those earlier VoxPopuli subsets and the current cleaned 628-record leaderboard partition has not been established. Its saved training-membership audit lists Common Voice, FLEURS, English Dialects, and SpeechOcean762; no LibriSpeech or Monsoon training partition appears in that audit. This is not a complete acoustic-overlap clearance, and inherited foundation-model exposure remains incompletely verified.

R15-0100 is proposed as a rollback before the two explicitly discussed continuation steps. Maintainer confirmation is requested on whether its remaining validation and selection history satisfies the requested checkpoint criterion. Its previous development and regression scores do not establish its result or rank on the current public-eight protocol. Any new result will identify this exact checkpoint and preserve the same evaluation settings.
